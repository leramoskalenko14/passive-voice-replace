Passive Voice

THE RULE

Present Simple Passive: am / is / are + V3

The rooms are cleaned every day.

Past Simple Passive: was / were + V3

The room was cleaned yesterday.

We use the passive voice when the action or the result is important — not the person who does it. The bread is baked every morning. (We don't say who bakes it — it isn't important.)
